13 · Floats

float pushes an element to the left or right side of its container and lets text wrap around it, like a picture in a magazine.

A. Wrapping text around an image

LEFT

This paragraph wraps around the box on the left. Floats were invented for exactly this: letting text flow around an image. The image is taken out of the normal flow and pushed to the side, and the lines of text shorten to make room for it. Once the text gets past the bottom of the image, it goes back to using the full width of the container again. Keep reading and you'll see the text return to full width right about now.

RIGHT

This one floats to the right instead. The margin on the floated box keeps the text from touching it. Without that margin, the words would sit right against its edge, which is much harder to read.
