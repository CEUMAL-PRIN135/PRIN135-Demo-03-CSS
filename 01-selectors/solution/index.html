D. Pseudo-elements

A pseudo-element styles a part of an element, or adds content that isn't in the HTML. It starts with two colons ::.

::before and ::after

::first-letter, ::first-line and ::selection

Once upon a time, a web developer wrote the same style on every single paragraph. Then they discovered selectors, and wrote it once instead. The first letter of this story is a drop cap, the first line is bold, and if you highlight any of this text with your mouse, the selection colour is orange. None of that is in the HTML: it's all pseudo-elements.
